Cash flow

Three separate metrics, never blended. Income is money entering from outside the tracked accounts. Spending counts each real purchase once, excluding card payments and internal transfers. Account movement is the raw per-account sum and includes transfers — it is not spending.

Recurring charges

Recurrence is a heuristic, not a fact from the institution. Three or more charges across three or more distinct months is the floor for medium; a stable amount on top of that is what makes it high. Two occurrences is not a subscription.

Leaks & opportunities

Each item is labelled with what kind of claim it is. A FACT is arithmetic on imported rows. An INFERENCE is a reading of them. A POSSIBLE OPPORTUNITY is a question for you, not a recommendation.
